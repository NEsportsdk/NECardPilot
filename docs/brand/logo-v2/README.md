# Vallective logo system — V2 Open V

Status: approved master direction and approved product integration.

## Core idea

The Open V is built from two equal, rounded strokes. It should feel precise, open and calm at large size while staying recognisable as a favicon. The left arm represents what the collector already owns; the violet right arm represents insight, value and forward motion.

## Master palette

- Midnight: `#07090D`
- Surface: `#10141C`
- Porcelain: `#F5F7FB`
- Vallective violet: `#7867FF`
- Violet highlight: `#A99BFF`
- Champagne editorial accent: `#CBAE72`

Champagne is an A+B supporting colour. It may frame editorial or social assets, but it does not replace violet inside the mastermark.

## Wordmark

The wordmark uses Geist Bold in uppercase with generous tracking. Keep the brand name as live text in ordinary product UI; use the supplied lockup artwork when the spacing between mark and wordmark must stay fixed.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space around the mastermark equal to the stroke width of one V arm.
- Do not use the standalone mark below 16 px.
- Use only the standalone mark in square or circular avatars below 96 px.
- Do not add charts, card details, shadows or gradients inside the V.

## Payoff

Master payoff: `Collect what matters. Know what it's worth.`

The earlier line `Know what you own. Know what it is worth.` remains suitable for explanatory campaign copy.
